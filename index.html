04 — About

Physics first. Useful science always.

JG
Environmental physicist · PhD
Université Grenoble Alpes

I build things that make urban climate science easier to use.

My research connects atmospheric modelling, Earth Observation and machine learning. I develop datasets and open-source software, run high-resolution simulations, and translate model output into questions that matter for cities: heat exposure, adaptation, thermal comfort and energy demand.

I have worked across academia and industry, including Université Grenoble Alpes, UNSW Sydney, GMV Aerospace & Defence and Deloitte Spain, and have taught machine learning for environmental fluid mechanics at Master’s level.
